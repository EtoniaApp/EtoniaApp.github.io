# Etonia Support

Etonia is a handwriting notes and study app for iPad, iPhone, and Android, with
an AI assistant that plots and writes directly on your page, plus a Study Desk
for guided learning and practice exams.

## Contact

when the problem happened.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my subscription on a new device?**
Open **Account**, then **Subscription**, and tap **Restore Purchases**. Sign in
with the same Apple or Google account you used before.

**How do I cancel a subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by the store where you bought them:
- **Apple App Store:** Open Settings, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, and
  choose Etonia.
- **Google Play:** Open the Play Store app, tap your profile icon, then
  **Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions**, and choose Etonia.

**Where are my notes stored?**
On your device. Turn on backup in **Account > Backup** to keep a copy in your
own Google Drive or, on Apple devices, your own iCloud.

**How do I delete my account?**
Open **Account**, then **Profile**, and tap **Delete account**. Your notes on
the device are kept. Cancel any App Store or Google Play subscription separately
in your store account settings.
