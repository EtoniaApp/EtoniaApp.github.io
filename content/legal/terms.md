PDF annotation, Goodnotes import via Google Drive, audio recording, and exports—are
free to use without ads.

## AI free trial, gift codes, and subscriptions

To use AI features (in the note editor, Study Desk, or Practice Studio), you
must sign in with an Apple or Google account.

- **Free trial:** Eligible signed-in accounts receive a one-time **2-week free
  trial** of unlimited **Haiku** and **Luna** (at low and medium reasoning
  effort). The 14-day trial starts on your first accepted AI request and is
  available once per account and once per device.
- **Gift codes:** Promotional gift codes, when offered, grant temporary AI token
  credit (usable for Haiku, Luna, Sonnet, Sol, and Flash) for a limited time,
  have no cash value, and do not extend the 2-week unlimited trial.
- **Paid subscriptions:** Etonia offers **Starter**, **Pro**, and **Max**
  plans, each billed monthly or yearly through your App Store or Google Play
  account. Every paid plan includes unlimited **Haiku** and **Luna** (at low
  and medium reasoning effort, subject to fair-use rate limits to protect
  service stability) plus a monthly or yearly AI token allowance for metered
  models (**Sonnet**, **Sol**, and **Flash** on Starter; plus frontier models
  **Opus**, **Argon**, and **Astra** on Pro and Max) and Study Desk semantic
  search embeddings.
- **Renewals and cancellation:** A subscription renews automatically unless you
  turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing
  period. Your App Store or Google Play account is charged for renewal within
  24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in
  your App Store or Google Play account settings. Prices are shown in the app
  before you buy.
- **Allowances and account binding:** Unused AI token allowances do not carry
  over to the next period and have no cash value. Subscriptions are bound to
  the Etonia account you use when purchasing.

before relying on them. Do not use Etonia's AI features for medical, legal, or
financial decisions.

You are responsible for what you submit to the AI features. You must not use
them to create unlawful, abusive, or harmful content, attempt to bypass rate
limits or billing controls, or automate or scrape our services.

## Your content and backups

Your notes, drawings, recordings, PDFs, and study materials belong to you.
Etonia stores them on your device, and in your own Google Drive or iCloud if you
turn on backup. Because we do not host a copy of your notes on our servers, you
are responsible for keeping backups of important work. See the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## Deleting your account

You can delete your Etonia account at any time in **Account > Profile > Delete
account**. Deleting your account removes your server-side account, plan, token,
and usage records, but does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play
subscription—cancel that separately in your store settings.
