01 AI Plot & Notation

From a circled equation to a plotted graph on your page.

Select your handwritten math and ask Etonia. Instead of replying only in a detached text box, Etonia draws coordinate axes, plots the curve with roots and extrema, and writes the steps in handwriting calibrated to your own strokes right beside your work.
